Roadmap to platinum

One playthrough is enough — about 200 hours. Do the story first and pick up what you can along the way, then clean up side questlines, activities and collectibles, and finish with robberies, money and the last stragglers. Only one trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude (the Chapter 1 meditation).

Guides are written for this tracker from the sources cited beside each trophy. Official trophy names, descriptions and grades are as PlayStation publishes them. No Rockstar Games artwork or logos are used. Works fully offline — progress is saved in your browser only.
